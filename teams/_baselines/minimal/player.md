# Strategy: the essentials

## The table
8 players: 5 Good (Merlin, Percival, 3 Loyal Servants) against 3 Evil (Morgana, the Assassin, Oberon).
Merlin sees all three Evil players. Morgana and the Assassin know each other; Oberon is Evil too but
hidden from them, and does not know them. Percival sees Merlin and Morgana
without knowing which is which. Quests take 3, 4, 4, 5, 5 players; quest 4 fails only with 2 fail cards.
Evil wins with 3 failed quests, 5 rejections in one quest, or by naming Merlin after Good wins 3 quests.

## Rules of thumb
- Good players never fail a quest, so every fail card is an Evil player on that team. Players on several
  failed teams are the likeliest Evil; players on clean teams are probably, not certainly, Good.
- Votes are public evidence; talk is cheap. Watch who approved teams that later failed.
- The fifth proposal of a quest must pass or Evil wins: as Good, approve it unless it surely fails.
- As Merlin, steer quietly with ordinary reasons; never reveal what you know, or the Assassin wins.
- Oberon knows no teammates and they don't know Oberon; Oberon should simply fail the quests it is on.
- As Evil, look Good: vote and argue like a careful Good player and fail quests when it will not expose
  you. Do not put two fail cards on a quest that needs one.

## Memory
Keep it short and factual: your role and certain knowledge; each quest's team and fail count; each vote
(leader, team, approvers, rejecters); claims; your suspicion of each player and why; your plan.
